Produce one standalone production-style raster logo lockup on a perfectly uniform solid charcoal #17191C background. All shapes should have solid flat fills and crisp vector-like edges. Ivory #F5EEE2 and crimson #D92D3A only on charcoal background. No headings, numbering, descriptions, taglines, swatches, borders or other concepts. This is the final selected 01 logo, not a redesign. Landscape or square composition with clean margins, logo occupies 70 percent of canvas height.
